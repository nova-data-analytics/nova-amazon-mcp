# Prompt library

Questions that work well against Nova MCP, grouped by the job you are doing. A longer, maintained set lives at [novadata.io/amazon-mcp-prompts](https://www.novadata.io/amazon-mcp-prompts).

## Weekly profit review

- Show me profit by SKU for the last 30 days after PPC.
- Which SKUs lost the most net profit versus last month, and why?
- What is my true TACoS by ASIN this quarter?
- Which SKUs are unprofitable at current price after all Amazon fees?
- Compare net margin this month versus the same month last year, by marketplace.

## Advertising cleanup

- Which campaigns spent over $100 last week with zero orders?
- Find search terms with ACoS under 25% and at least 3 orders, so I can promote them.
- Which search terms spent more than $50 with no conversions? Candidates for negatives.
- Which keywords have good ACoS but negative contribution margin?
- Show me TACoS trend by week for my top 20 SKUs.

## Inventory and supply

- What runs out of stock in the next three weeks across all marketplaces?
- Which SKUs are sitting on more than 120 days of cover?
- Which ASINs have the highest storage cost relative to their margin?

## Listing and buy box

- Which listings are suppressed, removed or not buyable right now?
- Where have I lost the buy box in the last 7 days?

## Rank

- Show the BSR trend for my top 10 ASINs over the last 90 days.
- Which of my ASINs improved rank the most this month?

## Diagnosis

- Sales dropped on ASIN B0XXXXXXX last week. What changed?
- Which marketplace deteriorated most this month, and which SKUs drove it?
